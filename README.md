# Haber–Bosch Process Simulation in DWSIM

## Project Overview
I simulated a chemical plant producing ammonia . The Project done on dwsim was to get me accustomed to reaction modelling in dwsim and troubleshooting making a process that gived a desired outcome 
The process consisted of a compressor, heater, equilibrium reactor, cooler and seperator . 

the project involved mass balances, chemical equilibrium, thermodynamic modelling,
effect of temperature and pressure on equilibrium,phase seperation , process simulation and optimisation 
The detailed engineering decisions, modelling and simulation are in the project report called 'Harber'
screenshots of the actual process and results are provided to showcase outcomes
